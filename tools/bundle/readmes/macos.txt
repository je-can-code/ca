Chef Adventure {VERSION}

To play:
1. Drag "Chef Adventure.app" into your Applications folder. Playing it straight from
   Downloads can stop the game from saving.
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS will refuse to open it, because this free game
   isn't notarized by Apple.
3. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to Chef Adventure. Confirm when it asks.
4. From then on it opens with a double-click, like any other app.

Your saves live inside the app. To find them, right-click "Chef Adventure.app", choose
"Show Package Contents", then open Contents, Resources, app.nw, and save. Copy that
"save" folder somewhere safe before replacing the app with a new version, then put it
back in the same place inside the new one.

Plug in a controller if you like. Have fun!
