Chef Adventure {VERSION}

To play:
1. Unzip this anywhere you like, such as your Desktop or Documents. Not Program Files,
   because the game can't save there.
2. Open the "Chef Adventure" folder and double-click "Chef Adventure.exe".

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's only because this
free game isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
It won't ask again.

Your saves are in the "package.nw\save" folder. To keep them when you move to a new
version, copy that "save" folder into the new version's "package.nw" folder.

Plug in a controller if you like. Have fun!
