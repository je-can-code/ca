## Family thesis

**Three magic sticks, three economies** — all ranged **`freeCombo`** casts on mains (whiff doesn’t block the next shot). Differentiate by **shape, tempo, MP/MRG/MMP**, and **offchain job** — not connect gates.

| Subgroup | Stat lean | Main | Offhand |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2H staff** | **MAT** (mod) · **MDF** (high) · low **MMP/MRG** | **Spray(3)** mid-range **`freeCombo`** | **Charge → aura** (MRG recovery + prof buff spine) |
| **1H wand** | **MAT** (high) · low **MDF/MMP** · mod **MRG** | **`freeCombo`** single bolt · low CD · long range | **Saturation** — **MAT** stack ramp |
| **Tome** | **MMP** (high) · low **MAT/MDF** · mod **MRG** | **`<direct>`** sentences · **`freeCombo`** | **Cast tempo** — tap fast / charge slow (Artillery synergy) |

**Charge semantics (family):** **charge = on button release** — never channeling. Hold → release → effect. (Guard and dash are the only hold behaviors.)

Legacy band **61–75** (staff / wand / tome) maps to **121–150** at migration.
