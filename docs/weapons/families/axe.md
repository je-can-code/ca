## Family thesis

**Axe is three questions about weight:**

| Subgroup | Tempo | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Hatchet** (1H) | **Slow** | How do I **outlast** while I chop? (self **buffers**) |
| **Glaive** (2H) | **Fast** | How do I **overwhelm** with tempo? (enemy bleed / crit) |
| **Mace** (2H) | **Deliberate** | How do I **unwrap** them for the **party**? (**−PDR** → **Exposed**) |

**Mace is the family's one blunt subgroup** — Hatchet and Glaive cut small and cut wide, and Mace does
not cut at all. It swings `Blunt` and carries `x Shields` on every rung, which is the mechanical form of
the `breaker` lot: its finisher is **SHATTER** and its first proficiency extension puts `<shieldDamage>`
on the whole main chain. Weapons are maces, picks and beaks — the things built to open armour rather
than get through it.

**Skill poison:** removed from **1H** skills by design; gear may still carry poison.

## Why the family is about weight `lineage`

The weight thesis is not an abstraction — it is **inherited from the prequel**, and Glaive's whole
craftable ladder exists to prove a twenty-year-old joke.

In *Jeremy & Robert*, the post-game morph atop the mountain on weird island — the **Raving Lunatic** —
wielded **The Styrofoam Axe**. The gag was entirely in the reveal: a ridiculous name attached to *cap
attack*. The lore held that the material was irrelevant. As long as it was an axe, he could kill you
with it, and he swung so fast that **cleaving the air did the killing** — the axe was only the shape he
held while tearing space apart, which is why it never chipped.

Jerald carries that. He and RL **fused permanently**, trading his magic — *element* and *morph* — for
what RL was, which is why he is a magicless frontliner who cannot look past what is in front of him
(see [`../../story-canon.md`](../../story-canon.md)). Jeremy wielded axes in the original too, so the
line runs **Jeremy → Raving Lunatic → Jerald**, and it is why the Academy's axe teacher calls him the
**Maestro of Axes** while he deflects that "the plot hasn't unveiled" it.

**So Glaive's ladder is a retrofitted proof.** Every craftable rung gets lighter and hits harder —
Leaden Poleaxe, Bulky Sagaris, Balanced Battleaxe, Nimble Labrys, Featherweight Bardiche, Gossamer
Halberd, and then a material with no mass at all. The Academy tutorial teaches exactly this physics
(*lighter means faster*) without knowing it is describing the weapon Jerald's own worst half used.

**This is why the glaive legendary is the one that breaks the myth-weapon rule.** Every other ⭐⭐⭐ and
slot-10 in the roster is drawn from world mythology. `"The Styrofoam Axe"` is not, because it does not
need to be — it is this game's own myth, and the ladder beneath it is the argument.
