## Family thesis

**Axe is three questions about weight:**

| Subgroup | Tempo | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **1H hatchet** | **Slow** | How do I **outlast** while I chop? (self **buffers**) |
| **2H battleaxe** | **Fast** | How do I **overwhelm** with tempo? (enemy bleed / crit) |
| **Breaker** | **Deliberate** | How do I **unwrap** them for the **party**? (**−PDR** → **Exposed**) |
