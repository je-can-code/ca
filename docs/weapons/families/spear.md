## Family thesis

**Spear is reach and rhythm** — how you control space and keep pressure going.

**Family direction:** **no passive “spear = crit”** on baseline skills. Subgroup defines the verb.

**`<freeCombo>` policy:** **removed from all melee spear lots** (pierce, mortar) — main chains advance on **connect**. **Throwing** (javelin lot) **will** use `<freeCombo>` (tempo rhythm; connect semantics do not fit ranged throws).

**Archetype spread:** **pierce** = AGI melee reach; **mortar** = **MAT + MP** (caster-friendly spear); **rend** = ranged **stack → RIP** pressure (`freeCombo` throws).
