## Combat skills

| # | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Curry Favor | Grants self positive rolls. Every chance proc Rupert makes now rolls multiple times — take any success. Fate bends toward him. |
| 2 | Court Disaster | Applies negative rolls to a target. Their chance procs must now succeed consecutively or they fail entirely. Fate abandons them. |
| 3 | Doom | Death sentence. Technically a percentage chance to apply. With Curry Favor active and Court Disaster on them, a formality. When the timer runs out, they die. |
