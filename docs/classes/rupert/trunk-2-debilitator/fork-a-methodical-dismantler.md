# Fork 2A — Methodical Dismantler

> Systematic, efficient stat stripping. Every hit removes something. By the time Avenge lands, there is nothing left to protect them.
> A tribute to Kore — a cat-humanoid who fought with claws. Rupert learned to fight like him. Honors him by doing it efficiently. RIP Kore.
>
> **Trunk:** [The Debilitator](./trunk.md)
>
> **SDP affinities:** Skirmisher, War Priest

---

## Native Equipment

- **Weapons:** arm, claymore
- **Armor:** medium armor, heavy armor, shield

---

## Combat skills

| # | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wing Clipper | Wide swipe targeting the legs. −50 move speed for several seconds. Applies **Wings Clipped**. They cannot reposition or flee. Engagement happens on Rupert's terms. |
| 2 | Shell Cracker | Thick line strike. Stuns for ~3 seconds AND reduces DEF 50%. Applies **Shell Cracked**. The stun is the bonus — the DEF reduction persists long after the stun ends. |
| 3 | Aura Burst | AoE pulse. Evaporates 50% MDF on all nearby enemies. Applies **Empty Aura**. Hits everything in range — every magic-using ally in the vicinity benefits immediately. |
| 4 | Avenge | **Signature.** One solid strike. Massive knockback. Stuns for ~6 seconds. Simultaneously applies **Shell Crack**, **Empty Aura**, and **Wings Clipped**. One hit, full dismantlement. For Kore. |
