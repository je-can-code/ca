# Fork 2A — Spear Chucker

> Maximum range. Stay away, build stacks, step in only for the RIP that detonates 100 javelins at once.
>
> **Trunk:** [Quicksilver](./trunk.md)
>
> **Natural weapons:** javelin (rend — stack ledger + RIP)
>
> **SDP affinities:** Artillery, Skirmisher

**Block size:** 20 (4 combat + 8–12 passive target, remainder spare)

---

## Combat skills (4)

| # | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spike Burst | Projectile that AoE Impales on impact — one throw, stacks spread across the group. Mechanically: attacks trigger AOE on hit. |
| 2 | Spike Trap | Plants an Impale zone at Jerald's feet. Backflip away and let enemies walk into it. |
| 3 | Spiky Clouds | Channeled. Locks Jerald down, rains javelins on a target zone repeatedly over the duration. Heavy Impale stacking. |
| 4 | Phantom Spikes | RIP-level damage but leaves Impaled stacks fully intact. Long cooldown. Cash a dividend without closing the ledger. |
