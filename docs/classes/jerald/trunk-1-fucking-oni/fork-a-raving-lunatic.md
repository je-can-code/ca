# Fork 1A — Raving Lunatic

> Blender cranked to 11. Fast, flurry, quantity of hits. Doesn't stop until everything is dead or he is.
> A living wood chipper that is outrageously mobile.
>
> **Trunk:** [Fucking Oni](./trunk.md)
>
> **Natural weapons:** dual blade (twist), fist glove (flow), 2H axe (battleaxe/cleave)
>
> **SDP affinities:** Berserker, Skirmisher

**Block size:** 20 (4 combat + 8–12 passive target, remainder spare)

---

## Combat skills (4)

| # | Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crimson Dive | Gap-closer. Dragoon-style jump lunge directly at a foe, dealing damage on arrival. |
| 2 | Tri-Viscerate | Triple slash in rapid succession. |
| 3 | Running with Scissors | Active buff. Grants CDR + damage boost for a duration. Not a stance. |
| 4 | Puree | **Signature.** Player-controlled whirlwind for several seconds at high speed. Shreds everything Jerald passes through. |
