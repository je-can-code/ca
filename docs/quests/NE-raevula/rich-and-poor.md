# Rich & Poor (relatively)

## Location
Raevula, just outside of the Comfy Bear Inn.

## About
The rich man's name is Vanderslop, while the average man's name is Peter. Vanderslop is obviously haughty taughty and a
huge fucking jerk, while Peter is... extremely average and normal.

Vanderslop's fiancee is Iris, the calculating heroine perusing the goods of The Withered Thorn. She actually hates
Vanderslop, but agreed to the engagement due to reasons, but pines for Peter for all his stable normalcy. He is the son
of Wyatt the Mayor of Raevula and flaunts his wealth in the faces of everyone he has the opportunity to.
(Character register: Saiki K's Saiko heir — outrageous wealth, thrown directly into peasant faces, at volume.)

Peter is the epitome of normalcy, a passionate farmer with a penchant for potatoes, constantly badgered by
Vanderslop who deems him a worthless peasant who should obey him on lineage alone (son of the mayor).

## Quests

### About progression through this quest chain
Vanderslop perceives Peter as a rival/inferior, and as a result, with every quest completed his rantings and ravings
towards Peter will escalate further and further. 

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-003`
Upon Jerald and Rupert's return after slaying Wyatt in the Forest of Dreams, the protagonists hear the two arguing
especially loudly; Vanderslop is fed up with Peter's insolence and so the quest chain is unlocked.

### 1a) Tribute: `???`
Vanderslop is absolutely livid with Peter's ignorance of his wealth. As a result, he demands tribute in the form of
delicious food. Specifically, he demands a salad. Of course, Peter being a farmer, he has some materials, but what kind
of salad can even be served to a fuckwad like Vanderslop? The `Wilted Wolftrap Salad`, naturally. Three of them.

### 1b) Helping Hand: `???`
With Vanderslop appeased for now, Peter recognizes your adventurer-like helpfulness, and has a humble request: his
tunic that he wears when farming is totally now a `Dilapidated Tunic`, having lost its `Verte` shimmer. Please take his
tunic and replenish its lost gloss and return with a `Verte Tunic` so he can farm in the harsh elements of Erocia once
again safely.

### 2a) Panegyric: `???`
Vanderslop is raging once again, unable to comprehend how Peter is not disappointed with himself for not being as
wealthy as Vanderslop. He laments "if you're truly happy, then prove it by showing me the fruits of your labor".
Despite the clearly flawed logic in Vanderslop's statements, it is up to Peter to yield the "fruits of his labor".
He is a farmer though, and while he can provide you the produce to make it, it seems it is up to you to cook another
dish for Vanderslop: specifically the `Seeing Jambalaya`. Again, three of them.

### 2b) Friend in Need: `???`
With Vanderslop sated once more, Peter can return to the original reason for which he came to Raevula in the first
place: to get some replacement shoes. Working long hours on the farm have resulted in wearing his soles to the bone.
His ask is simple: just some `Regular Shoes`- though coincidentally those are tier-5 shoes...

(Meta note: the `Verte Tunic` is mail-type and the `Regular Shoes` are cloth-type — meaning Peter, the epitome of
normalcy, is quietly bearing multiple `Equip X` passives. The most defensively versatile man in Erocia wears
anything he wants and uses it to farm potatoes. This is not a bug. This is the character.)

### 3a) Out-Farmed: `???`
The tributes stop working, so Vanderslop can't buy Peter's LAND (it's not for sale) — so he does the pettiest
possible thing money CAN do: he stands up a bigger, fancier, hired-labor farm operation right next door and
floods the Raevula market to make Peter's humble plot irrelevant. Can't make the peasant sad? Buy a superior
version of his entire life and park it in his eyeline. The duo's job is the banquet that launches this vanity
operation — Vanderslop hires them to cater the grand-opening, and the player clocks mid-prep exactly what this
party is celebrating.

Objectives:
- CRAFT/FETCH: 3× `Wilted Wolftrap Salad` + 3× `Seeing Jambalaya` (the full tribute menu, one last time)
- CRAFT/FETCH: 1× top-tier centerpiece dish (TBD — newly learnable at this chapter's kitchen tier)
- GO: deliver the banquet to Vanderslop's new operation

### 3b) The Harvest: `???`
Peter's counter to being out-scaled is the only move he has and the only one that matters: not more crop, *better*
crop — the best harvest of his life, delivered to the same market on the same day. He asks the duo for two days of
honest farm labor. Two ex-gods, potato-digging. (Jerald — canonically **"The Potato Expert,"** whose profile is
literally "a penchant for big mouthfuls of potatoes" — is genuinely, disturbingly good at it. Peter, penchant for
potatoes himself, recognizes a kindred spirit across the species barrier. Nobody else understands what is passing
between these two men. They do not explain. They simply dig.)

Objectives:
- SLAY: 8× `Wolftrap` (+ 2× `Bloodtrap`?) infesting the plots — pest control first
- FETCH: 20× potatoes via plot interactions on Peter's farm map
- (Optional TIME spice: within two in-game days — the deadline made literal)

### 4b) His Keeper: `???`
It is PETER — of course it is Peter — who asks the duo to go check on him. The man Vanderslop spent the entire
chain humiliating is the only person in Raevula who noticed he stopped eating. ("He demanded three salads a week.
A man doesn't just stop demanding salads.") The duo finds Vanderslop wherever the Maker likes his rock-bottom:
unwashed, unwealthy, and — for the first time in the chain — honest.

Objectives:
- GO: find Vanderslop (destination — rock-bottom venue TBD; the Red Baron's darkest corner is right there)

### 5) The Fork: `???`
The conclusion, player-nudged through the dialogue of 4a/4b onward:

Objectives (redemption path):
- FETCH: deliver Peter's restored `Verte Tunic` — yes, THE tunic from 1b, handed down. The chain's tribute
  economy ends with Peter giving Vanderslop the shirt off his back, literally, for farm work.
- GO: the farm, first day of labor (destination; dialogue fork resolves here)

Objectives (ruin path):
- none — he asks for nothing, which is the whole problem. Dialogue only.

- **Redemption:** stripped of everything, Vanderslop takes the only work on offer — Peter's farm. The heir digs
  potatoes. He is terrible at it, then adequate, then — horribly — *proud* of a harvest. His lineage claim in the
  succession question (Frederick's chain) gets withdrawn honestly or re-founded on actual worth. He never stops
  being insufferable; he becomes insufferable *about soil quality*.
- **Ruin:** he doubles down — presses the mayorship claim as a naked grab, is rejected by a town that remembers
  his father, and calcifies into the bitter man his money used to hide.

Either way, the triangle resolves — from Iris's side, in
[her own doc](../SW-raevula/calculating-heroine.md) (she chooses herself first, then Peter); from this side, the
engagement's end is a line item in the collapse. Peter ends the chain with the farm secure, the right partner,
and zero idea he won anything, because he never thought he was competing.

#### After effects of "The Fork"
- The succession thread receives Vanderslop's resolution, whichever it was.
- One quiet beat, unspoken by anyone: the duo spends the whole chain assuming Peter is the joke — and exits it
  having met the only man in Erocia who already has what they're questing toward. Protagonist-grade capabilities,
  potato-farmer's life, completely at peace. They don't have the words for it yet. The ending does.

### Coda) The Deed: `???`
The vanity farm survives its owner's bankruptcy — and goes on the market (probate sale; Frederick the executor,
if the succession interlock holds). And who on this continent is drowning in gold, given that every enemy drops
it and almost nothing costs it? **The duo. The player buys the farm.**

- Price: **~1,000,000G** (ballpark) — the game's first true GOLD SINK. Twenty hours of
  monster wallets finally have somewhere to go; Gilbert's buyback economy retroactively becomes farm savings.
- **Hire your farmer** for a paltry sum: **Peter** (available always), and/or **Vanderslop** (redemption path
  only — the heir, working YOUR farm, for pocket change; on the ruin path he's too bitter to ask). If both are
  hired: the rivals farm side by side, and the output improves, and neither of them will discuss it.
- **The loop:** return daily (TIME-driven) for a fresh produce crate — potatoes guaranteed (two experts on
  payroll), seasonal extras to taste.
- The register of the reward, worth noticing: the chain ABOUT wealth thrown in peasant faces ends by handing
  the player Vanderslop's exact position — landowner, employer, rich beyond need — and quietly watches what
  they do with it. No prompt. No judgment. Just a farm, two hirelings, and a mirror.

Objectives:
- GO: the vanity farm / the probate sale (destination)
- PAY: 1,000,000G (the deed)
- HIRE: Peter and/or Vanderslop (dialogue; availability per the Fork's outcome)
