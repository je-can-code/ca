# The Cat-woman Hybrid

## Location
Raevula, wandering the northwest section aimlessly, as is her nature.

## About
The Cat Lady's name is Nyancy. Fourth-wall aware in the most self-serving way possible: "Don't I look like a
character that should be joining your party or something?" She is convinced she is party-member material. She is
not. Nobody has the heart to tell her (Jerald absolutely has the heart to tell her; Rupert keeps stopping him).
Tier: FLAVOR — pure comedy, tiny chain. Interlock: the only being in Raevula who can translate
[Mittens](../SE-raevula/hungry-kitty.md).

**Buried truth `LOCKED (2026-07-24, The Erasures — see story-canon)`:** she isn't delusional. **She's the v1
protagonist** — the punch-ring cat girl from the first erased timeline (~2008). Her signature line is a memory,
not a bit. The audition chain below reads completely differently in this light: a former lead, trying to get her
old job back, in the timeline that finally stuck. The comedy stays comedy. The player who knows, knows.

**Reward family: cat nonsense.**

## Quests

### 1) Audition One: `nyancy-001`
She demands a formal party audition. Her demonstrated skills: knocking valuable items off high surfaces,
ignoring direct instructions, and landing on her feet from a height of one (1) fence. Verdict: no. Her takeaway:
"So there's an opening."

### 2) Audition Two: `nyancy-002`
She has trained. New skills: sitting in boxes regardless of box size, staring at a wall with enormous purpose,
and a sixty-percent-accurate pounce. Verdict: no. Her takeaway: "I could feel you hesitate."

### 3) The Final Audition: `nyancy-003`
She performs an unbroken three-minute routine of actual, genuine grace — and then walks away mid-verdict because
she lost interest, which is the most feline possible outcome. Reward: she teaches "Cat's Grace" (a small
evasion/luck passive) out of magnanimity, since "you clearly need it more than me."

#### After effects of "The Final Audition"
- Nyancy's idle dialogue permanently shifts to treating the duo as her understudies.
- She will now translate Mittens' inner monologue on request. The player will regret this.
