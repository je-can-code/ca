# The Snotty Little Kid

## Location
Raevula, the northwest section, planted in front of her own house like a one-child garrison. "You may not enter
my abode." Chapters 1–4: immovable.

## About
The snotty little kid's name is Silia. Surface: maximum snot, zero fear, guards her door against two visible
demigods without blinking. **Buried truth (pitch):** her father is a miner — one of the ones the deep shaft
didn't give back. She holds the house because someone has to keep it exactly right "for when dad's back." The
snot is armor. She is the PERSONAL channel of the Subterranean whisper doctrine (Amy is the gossip channel) —
and the game's Nanako: a kid processing a loss she doesn't have words for, growing up faster than she should.

**Reward family: keepsakes** — small, sentimental, mechanically light.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: chapter 4 (the house opens)
In chapter 4 she finally lets the duo in — not because she trusts them, but because they're the only people in
town who go where she can't.

### 1) The Fort: `???`
Inside, the house is... kept. Tidy. A place at the table set. She wants help gathering "supplies dad likes" —
miner's rations, lamp oil, pearl salt. The duo assumes it's a game. The player slowly realizes it isn't.

#### After effects of "The Fort"
- Silia's exterior dialogue softens by one single degree. One.

### 2) Deep Delivery: `???`
> Gate: snakerope (the deep entrances). This is a Subterranean breadcrumb — door, never contents.

She asks the duo to deliver her father's lamp to "where he's working — the deep part." Adventurers can go where
kids can't. What they find at the deep entrance — and whether anything of her father's story is answered down
there — is **OPEN and the Maker's alone.** (Options range from a marker at the shaft mouth to something found
during the Sloth dungeon itself. Do not resolve cheaply.)

#### After effects of "Deep Delivery"
- Reward: the "Miner's Charm" (accessory — modest light-radius/darkness utility; his lamp, shared).
- Whatever truth the Maker chooses lands here, and her ending dialogue state with it.
