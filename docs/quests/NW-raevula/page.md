# The Page

## Location
Raevula, the northwest section — the mayor's mansion, which is closed off from the end of chapter 1 until
chapter 4. Frederick maintains it alone in the interim.

## About
The Page's name is Frederick. He served Wyatt — and per the rich-and-poor doc, he "had concerns" about what the
mayor was planning for the previous adventurers. **Buried truth:** he ushered every one of those adventurers
through the front door himself. He knew something was wrong. He said nothing, because pages don't question
mayors. He has been maintaining a dead monster's estate ever since, because the alternative is admitting there's
nothing left to serve.

**The thread this chain carries: RAEVULA HAS NO MAYOR.** The player killed him in chapter 1 and the town has
never once addressed it. Frederick's chain is where the town finally processes what the protagonists did — the
"everyone is the protagonist of their own story" thesis applied to civic consequences.

**Reward family: the estate** — Wyatt lore, mansion access, and (capstone) player housing.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: chapter 4 (the mansion reopens)

### 1) The Estate: `frederick-001`
Frederick asks the duo to help inventory the mansion — the town wants answers about its mayor, and nobody else
will set foot in the place. Sweep the wings, catalog what's found. Among it: Wyatt's records. How long was he the
Sin of Gluttony? What was he before? (Feeds the `OPEN` Wyatt/Lucian-origins thread in story-canon — whatever the
Maker decides, this is where the evidence lives.)

#### After effects of "The Estate"
- Mansion interior accessible; loot recovered; Wyatt lore entries discovered.

### 2) The Question of Governance: `frederick-002`
The inventory forces the issue: the town needs a mayor. **Vanderslop presses his lineage claim** (interlock:
[rich-and-poor](../NE-raevula/rich-and-poor.md) — his ruin-or-redemption fork lands HERE). Frederick, who served
one monster, refuses to install another by default. The player's involvement in the chain nudges the outcome
(Vanderslop redeemed vs. rejected; a council; Frederick himself drafted against his will — option space open).

#### After effects of "The Question of Governance"
- Raevula visibly gains whatever governance the outcome produces; NPC dialogue town-wide updates.

### 3) The West Wing: `frederick-003`
Capstone: nobody in town will live in the mansion — the mayor ate people there. Frederick, with whatever
authority the town has left, grants the duo the West Wing: **the room they were locked in becomes theirs.**
PLAYER HOUSING. The prison becomes the home.

#### After effects of "The West Wing"
- The West Wing becomes a player base (rest point, storage, trophy display — scope to taste).
- Frederick's final dialogue state: a man who served something worth serving, at last. His words.
