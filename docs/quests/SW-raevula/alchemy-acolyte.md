# The Alchemy Acolyte

## Location
Raevula, the southwest section — behind the counter of The Withered Thorn, hood up, selling alchemy goods.
(Census note: the "hooded girl" wondering where the alchemist went, pre-rescue, is presumably her standing
outside — same person, pre-Leo's-return dialogue state.)

## About
The hooded alchemy acolyte's name is Alice. She runs the Withered Thorn's counter, keeps the hood up indoors,
and has opinions about reagent quality that border on religious. **Buried truth `LOCKED (2026-07-24, The
Erasures — see story-canon)`:** she is **the v4 protagonist** — Alice of CAEX, who had a Path, a love interest
(Xavier), and a route where she conquered Heaven. The hood hides former main-character status. She survived the
gent's fourth erasure and lives quietly in the timeline that stuck, selling reagents in someone else's story.
Whether she ever says any of this out loud — or whether the hood simply stays a boundary the game never
violates — is the Maker's call on delivery, not on truth.

**Reward family: alchemy goods** — shop tiers, rare catalysts (the alchemy-side mirror of Gilbert's economy
chain, scoped smaller).

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: Leo's rescue (`main-004`)
The Withered Thorn's chain opens once its backroom alchemist is home.

### 1) The Catalog: `alice-001`
Alice wants the shop's reagent catalog rebuilt to her standards. The fetch list deliberately tours optional
zones (zone doctrine: chains advertise the continent) — a reagent from the cliffs, one from the forest depths,
one from wherever the player dares.

#### After effects of "The Catalog"
- Withered Thorn stock tier up; rare catalysts rotate in.

### 2) The Hood: `alice-002`
Trust beat. Content depends entirely on which buried-truth option the Maker picks — or the option (c) version:
the duo finally just asks; she says "no"; Rupert, of all people, respects it completely; the friendship is the
reveal. Reward: a unique catalyst ("Acolyte's Favor") and her first unhooded... no. Her first *laugh*. Hood stays.

#### After effects of "The Hood"
- Post-Nimbus (if option (a)): her dialogue about the master resolves alongside [Leo's](./alchemist-bard.md).
