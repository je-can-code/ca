# The Alchemist Bard

## Location
Raevula, the southwest section — the backroom of The Withered Thorn, post-rescue (`main-004`). Before that: the
Forlorn Basin, nearly a slime's lunch.

## About
The alchemist-slash-bard's name is Leonarado de Spillbert III — "but you can call me Leo." A bard who turned
alchemist and, by his own cheerful admission, sucked at alchemy. His ukelele is the player's first tool (stolen,
technically; he's decided not to press the point). His canon line is the thesis wearing a party hat: **"Can you
folks not treat me like I'm some kind of side character or something?!"**

**The load-bearing fact:** Leo studied under a legendary master — the world-famous alchemist whose farewell note
sits in the Withered Thorn attic, who left for "a new world." That master is the **Sin of Pride**, waiting in
Nimbus. Leo's personal chain and the ch4 main story are two halves of one arc: the student in the shadow, and
what the shadow turned out to be.

**Reward family: alchemy formulae** — the Journal of Alchemy pages (already specced in
`../../unlockables/alchemy-pages.md`) and, capstone, his own original work.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-004` (the rescue)

### 1) The Journal of Alchemy: `side-009`
Already authored: six pages, six locations (the 4a–8a bird, the lakeside view, Highwater, the forest cliffs,
the toothpick ledge, and the low-tide cove, added 2026-10-26). Each page traded for formulae, and the sixth completes
the journal with the Philosopher's Stone. This doc anchors the existing quest to his arc: every page
is in the master's handwriting, and Leo's commentary while decoding them quietly builds the portrait of the woman
who left him behind. **Built 2026-11-22**: the duo read each page aloud and Leo reacts, six beats from her handwriting
to "Perfection is the only acceptable failure", and the formulas per page are in
[`alchemy-pages.md`](../../unlockables/alchemy-pages.md#the-exchange-as-built-2026-11-22).

### 2) The Note in the Attic: `leo-001`
The player has read it (Map035). Leo hasn't — not in years, not since it stopped hurting enough to avoid. Sit
with him while he reads it again. What he takes from it before Nimbus: "she went somewhere I couldn't follow."
He's wrong about how, and the game knows it.

### 3) The Student: `leo-002`
> Gate: post-Nimbus (Pride defeated).

The news reaches Raevula. The master didn't leave for a grander world — she *became* the grandest thing she could
imagine and it ate her, and two strangers put her down on a floating rock above the peaks. Leo takes the beat.
And then — the arc's answer — he goes back to the bench and brews the thing he could never manage while the
shadow was over him. It works. **"Leo's Original"** enters the game: a top-tier alchemy line the master never
made, because the master never could — pride doesn't experiment; it performs.

#### After effects of "The Student"
- "Leo's Original" formulae unlock (top-tier alchemy). **Tier 6 gems are Leo's Original** (Jeremy, 2026-11-22: "the
  6th tier will be a bit more amazing than just 'one more step on the ladder'"), so the journal's ladder stops at V.
- **The Originals, designed 2026-11-23 (not built):** each one is its Gem V's stat at **+50%** plus a **+10**
  secondary. Every secondary is a raw number or a stat contested by a counter-stat, never a percent that tops out at
  100, because gear refines endlessly. The recipe is that Gem V, plus the tier V monster part carrying the secondary,
  on the **Philosopher's Stone** as the tool:

  | Original | Secondary | Ingredients |
  |---|---|---|
  | Life | HRG +10 | Life Gem V + Immortal Liver |
  | Magi | MRG +10 | Magi Gem V + Flowing Pelt |
  | Power | CDM +10 | Power Gem V + Executioner Tooth |
  | Endurance | GRD +10 | Endurance Gem V + Spherical Stone |
  | Sorcery | HIT +10 | Sorcery Gem V + Live Stinger |
  | Resist | MEV +10 | Resist Gem V + Radiant Core |
  | Finesse | CRI +10 | Finesse Gem V + Razor Fangs |
  | Luck | CEV +10 | Luck Gem V + Lethal Talon |

  The joke is Jeremy's: the legendary stone finally gets used, for something "kinda mundane sounding (taking an
  already existing gem and slapping an item on it that is adjacent to make a better gem... uncreative lmaoooo, but on
  the nose for Leo sucking at everything he does)". It works anyway, which is the point.
- His dialogue drops the self-deprecation. Not all of it. The right amount.
- The side character line pays off: the game makes him, on screen, the protagonist of his own story. Thesis,
  delivered by ukelele guy.
