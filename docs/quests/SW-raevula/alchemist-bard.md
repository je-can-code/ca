# The Alchemist Bard

## Location
Raevula, the southwest section — the backroom of The Withered Thorn, post-rescue (`main-004`). Before that: the
Forlorn Basin, nearly a slime's lunch.

## About
The alchemist-slash-bard's name is Leonarado de Spillbert III — "but you can call me Leo." A bard who turned
alchemist and, by his own cheerful admission, sucked at alchemy. His ukelele is the player's first tool (stolen,
technically; he's decided not to press the point). His canon line is the thesis wearing a party hat: **"Can you
folks not treat me like I'm some kind of side character or something?!"**

**The load-bearing fact:** Leo studied under a legendary master — the world-famous alchemist whose farewell note
sits in the Withered Thorn attic, who left for "a new world." That master is the **Sin of Pride**, waiting in
Nimbus. Leo's personal chain and the ch4 main story are two halves of one arc: the student in the shadow, and
what the shadow turned out to be.

**Reward family: alchemy formulae** — the Journal of Alchemy pages (already specced in
`../../unlockables/alchemy-pages.md`) and, capstone, his own original work.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-004` (the rescue)

### 1) The Journal of Alchemy: `side-009`
Already authored: five pages, five locations (the 4a–8a bird, the lakeside view, Highwater, the forest cliffs,
the toothpick ledge). Each page traded for formulae. This doc anchors the existing quest to his arc: every page
is in the master's handwriting, and Leo's commentary while decoding them quietly builds the portrait of the man
who left him behind.

### 2) The Note in the Attic: `???`
The player has read it (Map035). Leo hasn't — not in years, not since it stopped hurting enough to avoid. Sit
with him while he reads it again. What he takes from it before Nimbus: "he went somewhere I couldn't follow."
He's wrong about how, and the game knows it.

### 3) The Student: `???`
> Gate: post-Nimbus (Pride defeated).

The news reaches Raevula. The master didn't leave for a grander world — he *became* the grandest thing he could
imagine and it ate him, and two strangers put him down on a floating rock above the peaks. Leo takes the beat.
And then — the arc's answer — he goes back to the bench and brews the thing he could never manage while the
shadow was over him. It works. **"Leo's Original"** enters the game: a top-tier alchemy line the master never
made, because the master never could — pride doesn't experiment; it performs.

#### After effects of "The Student"
- "Leo's Original" formulae unlock (top-tier alchemy).
- His dialogue drops the self-deprecation. Not all of it. The right amount.
- The side character line pays off: the game makes him, on screen, the protagonist of his own story. Thesis,
  delivered by ukelele guy.
