# The Adventurous Guy and Wooed Gal

## Location
Raevula, the southwest section, outside their house, being in love at maximum volume.

## About
The guy and gal are named Silver and Kayla. Canon, from their shipped lines: "Baby, when I hit level 44, I'm
gonna take you on an adventure to the—" / "I can't wait until you reach level 44!" Silver is approximately level
four. The dream is approximately forty levels away.

**Buried truth (pitch, and it's a good one): Kayla is already past level 44.** She grinds at night (TIME-plugin
content) while he sleeps, and sandbags in public — because the dream is HIS, and she'd rather wait at his pace
than arrive without him. The "wooed gal" is the strongest civilian in Raevula and nobody knows.

Tier: FLAVOR-plus, comedy-sweet. This is the game's one uncomplicated love story — the couple that simply makes
it — and given the author's romantic history, the roster has earned exactly one of these.

**Reward family: small and sentimental.**

## Quests

### 1) Training Montage: `silverkayla-001`
Silver asks the two visibly godlike strangers for coaching. It goes catastrophically. He improves from level 4
to level 6 and celebrates like it's a world title. Kayla watches from the porch with the specific smile of
someone holding a beautiful secret.

### 2) The Night Shift: `silverkayla-002`
> Night content (TIME).

The player, out at night, catches Kayla mid-grind — soloing bearcats with terrifying efficiency. The scene plays;
she doesn't apologize; she explains (see Buried truth) and asks the duo to keep it. **Choice:** keep her secret
(the only correct answer, but the game lets Jerald *almost* ruin it first).

### 3) Level 44: `silverkayla-003`
Silver hits it. The adventure is ON: a trip to a view he's always wanted to show her (destination to taste —
Lakeside? the cliffs?). The duo's final job: shadow the trip unseen and quietly make sure nothing interrupts it.
Two demigods spend an afternoon secretly bodyguarding a picnic. Nothing goes wrong. That's the point.

#### After effects of "Level 44"
- Reward: the **Promise Ring** (accessory — minor paired bonus when both equipped-slot conditions met, or a
  small party-wide morale buff; scope to taste).
- Their idle dialogue advances to planning the NEXT adventure. Level 60 this time. Kayla can't wait.
