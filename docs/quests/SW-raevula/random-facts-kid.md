# The Random Facts Kid

## Location
Raevula, moving swiftly and randomly through the southwest section. Catching him is part of the content.

## About
This kid's name is Fortuitus (fortune, luck, RNG made flesh). Opening line, canon: "Did you know I know stuff?"
He does, in fact, know stuff. His facts are a shuffle of genuine mechanics hints, real named-monster locations,
and complete nonsense — delivered with identical confidence, and the player cannot tell which is which. (Design
option, dealer's choice: ALL of them are true, including the absurd ones, which is funnier and eventually
becomes a meta-reward for players who tested them.)

Differentiation: [Amy](../N-raevula/rumor-loving-lady.md) deals in people and places; Fortuitus deals in
monsters and mechanics. Two info-brokers, no overlap, both children of the whisper doctrine.

**Reward family: hints/lore-drip** — hunt pointers, mechanic reveals.

## Quests

### 1) Catch Me If You Can (I–V): `fortuitus-001`
He won't talk standing still. Catch him (he's FAST — pixel-movement chase content, the movement system as
minigame). Each catch pays one fact. Facts escalate from "bearcats hate mint" to a genuine named-anomaly location
(Hunting Lord interlock) to, eventually, one very quiet, very real fact that doesn't sound like the others —
(free slot: a Nare mention, a Subterranean whisper, or whatever the Maker wants planted in the mouth of the one
NPC nobody fact-checks).

#### After effects
- Caught facts get logged somewhere reviewable (codex/journal entry per catch).
- Final catch: he admits, winded, that nobody's ever caught him five times. "You must REALLY like facts." He then
  delivers his favorite one for free. It should be the dumbest one in the game, and it should also be true.
