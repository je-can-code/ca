# The Calculating Heroine

## Location
Raevula, the southwest section — perusing the goods of The Withered Thorn, running numbers.

## About
The calculating heroine's name is Iris. Pair-doc with [rich-and-poor](../NE-raevula/rich-and-poor.md) — same
triangle, her side. Canon: Vanderslop's fiancée, hates him, agreed to
the engagement "due to reasons," pines for Peter and his stable normalcy. **Buried truth (pitch):** the
"calculating" was never coldness — it was survival math. The engagement was an equation she solved under duress
(the "reasons": family debt, obligation — exact terms OPEN), and she has been quietly running the numbers on her
own escape ever since. She window-shops alchemy goods because pricing volatile reagents is how she keeps her
mind sharp. The town reads her as mercenary. She's actually the most disciplined person in it.

**Reward family: appraisal/economics** — market insight, item appraisal, drop-value intel.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-003` (alongside rich-and-poor's opening)

### 1) Cost-Benefit: `iris-001`
Iris hires the duo for oddly specific errands: price checks at Gilbert's stall (interlock), weight-verification
of trade goods, one discreet appraisal. She pays exactly fair market rate, to the coin. The player is helping
her build a ledger. She doesn't say what it's for. It's an exit fund.

#### After effects of "Cost-Benefit"
- **Monsterpedia odds unlock:** the Omnipedia's bestiary starts displaying **drop percentages (and SDP drop
  chances)** for observed monsters. It's precisely her gift, taught: the duo doesn't get sharper eyes — they
  get her habit of *reading the probabilities*. (Real utility in the two economies this game actually runs on.)

### 2) The Variables: `iris-002`
> Interlock gate: the succession thread ([page.md](../NW-raevula/page.md)) — Vanderslop's estate destabilizes.

The dead mayor's money stops flowing and Vanderslop's leverage evaporates — and with it, the "reasons." Her
equation's constraints just changed. She asks the player one question, genuinely: not "what should I do" but
**"what did YOU do, when you got strong enough that nobody could make you do anything?"**

And the answers are exactly what they are:

> J: "Sword in ass, obviously."
> R: "Ensured they regretted their life decisions for all eternity."

Ridiculous. Truly meaningless at face value. And then her gift does what it does — the calculation engine that
prices reagents and builds exit-fund ledgers turns itself on the noise:

> *(deep narrator voice)* **"What Iris hears:"**
> J: *Follow through with YOUR truth — only you know it.*
> R: *Plan for a future where you are free, and finally happy.*

And it clicks. She runs with it — straight into quest 3.

(Note the third layer, because it's load-bearing: her translations aren't generous spin — they're ACCURATE.
"Sword in ass" IS Jerald: total follow-through on exactly what he is, no apology, ever. Eternal-regret-planning
IS Rupert: the strategist's long horizon. The ridiculous answers were honest self-descriptions all along; Iris
is simply the only person on the continent whose gift is parsing signal out of these two. The duo never breaks
character, never gets wise, never knows they changed a life. The one-time narrator device belongs to her alone —
do not reuse it for anyone else; parsing IS her superpower, and this box is its one perfect firing.)

### 3) Recalculation: `iris-003`
She solves it — and the solve matters: **she chooses herself first.** Breaks the engagement on her own terms,
settles the "reasons" with her own ledger, and only THEN — free, unowed, unpressured — walks over to Peter and
his potatoes. Not rescued. Not won. Recalculated. (Whether Peter fumbles the moment adorably: yes.)

#### After effects of "Recalculation"
- The triangle resolves town-wide (rich-and-poor states advance; Vanderslop's fork feeds the succession thread).
- **Gilbert's black market unlocks.** She can't grant discounts and item values are meaningless in a one-vendor
  economy — but she CAN teach the duo to *see value where nobody's pricing it*:

  > J: "Hey fatty, I wanna buy that." *(points at a ridiculously priceless artifact)*
  > G: "What? Why would you wanna buy that?"
  > R: "Iris told us it's probably useful, and that you'd give us a second shop scene if we pointed at that."
  > G: *(grumbles about kids not being able to keep their yaps shut as he opens the secondary
  > "rare accessory" shop scene)*

  The rare-accessory shop = the second real gold sink (alongside the farm deed), routed through the only vendor
  who takes gold — and quest 1's price-checks at his stall retroactively become the setup: she'd been auditing
  his inventory the whole time.
- Idle dialogue, final state: still calculating. Now it's a garden plot and a wedding budget. Same rigor.
