# The Foreign Gentleman

## Location
Raevula, the middle-south section (Map336), outside a house he's been renting "temporarily."

## About
The foreign gentleman's name is Satoru. Canon (per the Maker, 2026-07-24): he is traveling **from Nare**, and
**passed through Erocia Kingdom proper** on his way here — hence his line comparing quaint little Raevula
unfavorably to the Kingdom.

Read that with chapter 3 in mind, because it makes him three things at once:

1. **The impossible witness.** He walked through a *living* Kingdom — recently, from his frame of reference —
   and the player will stand in its ancient ruins in chapter 3. Satoru's casual testimony is civilian-grade
   *evidence of the regression-aging*: the timeline wrinkle, walking around town with opinions about
   architecture. **He doesn't know it fell — he left it standing.**
2. **Nare's third receipt** — after the rich man and the naren lady knight — and the strongest: he *traveled the
   road*. Nare is reachable. The route exists. (Epilogue implications noted; the onsen has a supply line.)
3. Still the tragedy link: whatever business, ties, or people he left along that road — a return trip he keeps
   postponing — died in the aging. He plans to go back the way he came. The way he came no longer exists.

(His name means "to know." The man who doesn't know is named Satoru. This was probably an accident. The game
keeps doing this.)

**Interlock — the Kingdom-expat cluster:** the "royal erocian man" drinking at the Red Baron ("even a royal is
driven to drink sometimes"), the **naren lady knight** at the inn "on royal business" (visiting a kingdom that
can't answer), and the royal-looking papers. Three flavor NPCs and Satoru = one scattered story: the people who
left before it happened.

**Reward family: story** — this chain pays in truth, not loot. Maybe one heirloom.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-007`
The chain only opens once the player has seen the Fallen Kingdom with their own eyes. Before that, Satoru is
just flavor. After it, every line he says is a knife.

### 1) Letters Home: `???`
Satoru learns the duo are travelers and asks the simplest favor in the game: carry a letter home for him. The
player is holding a letter addressed to a place they know is graves. **Choice:** tell him now, or take the letter
in silence. (The duo, characteristically, does not grasp the weight. The player does. That gap is the whole
design — same trick as Gilbert's graves.)

### 2) What Remains: `???`
However quest 1 resolved, the truth arrives — from the player, or from the naren knight's investigation
converging on the same silence. Satoru asks for one thing: escort him there, or bring him something back. What he
does with what he learns — a memorial in Raevula, a decision to stay, something else — is **OPEN, the Maker's.**

#### After effects of "What Remains"
- The expat cluster's dialogue states all resolve together (the royal at the pub, the knight at the inn).
- Whatever Satoru builds or becomes stands in Raevula permanently.
- Optional reward: a Kingdom heirloom (accessory), given not as payment but because "someone should carry
  something of it that isn't a sword."
