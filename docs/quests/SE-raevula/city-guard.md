# The City Guard

## Location
Raevula, the southeast section — "patrolling" the Metal Petal, which is to say, standing in it.

## About
The city guard's name is Cecil (a knight, stationed a stone's throw from The Red Baron — if you know, you know).
Surface: earnest, mildly redundant, guards a weapons shop in a town whose biggest threat died in chapter 1.
**Buried truth:** he's the town's ONLY guard, and the town has no government to report to (see
[the Page](../NW-raevula/page.md) — succession interlock). He patrols the Metal Petal because it's where the
weapons are, and his quiet, entirely private fear is that the next Wyatt walks in and he's the only thing between
the town and it. He knows exactly how that fight goes.

Tier: FLAVOR-plus. **Reward family: guarding** — the guard teaches guarding (proficiency system tie).

## Quests

### 1) Drills: `cecil-001`
Cecil, having watched two demigods stroll through town, swallows his pride and asks them to train him. The beat:
a guard/parry-focused sparring minigame where the PLAYER demonstrates and Cecil learns. It goes badly, then less
badly, then — once — genuinely well, and his face when he holds a parry against Jerald is the whole reward.
(Mechanically also: a guard-proficiency bump or a small unique guard skill for the player. Teaching teaches the
teacher.)

#### After effects of "Drills"
- Cecil's idle stance animation changes — he stands like someone who trained.

### 2) The Patrol Route: `cecil-002`
> TIME plugin showcase — night content.

He invites the duo on his actual patrol: the full circuit of Raevula, at night. Nothing attacks. That's not the
point. The point is what the town looks like at night, who's awake (Treis at the pub; the piano man playing to an
empty room; lights in Silia's window), and one man walking the whole of it, every night, unpaid and unasked,
because somebody should.

#### After effects of "The Patrol Route"
- A couple of night-only town secrets get pointed out (small loot/flavor).
- If the succession chain concludes, whoever governs formally recognizes his post. He'd never have asked.
