# The Smith's Apprentice

## Location
Raevula, the southeast section — behind the counter of The Metal Petal, running the entire shop, thanks for
noticing.

## About
The apprentice's name is Viskra. Canon establishes her on arrival: sold out of pre-mades, gramps at the pub, one
person holding the store together with sass and spite. Sunny, sharp-tongued, and — the load-bearing trait —
**willing to GO HARD, ALL THE WAY,** on anything she decides to master. She was never taught "the old ways,"
because nobody teaches those first; you teach ingots, then swords. What she knows about smithing greatness is
whatever Viktor manages to actually SAY to her. Remember that.

**The design thesis (for me):** this chain is the doublespeak tragedy. The world is bathed in people not saying
what they mean, and here is the translation: a master says "soul" meaning *heart*, and his brightest pupil —
diligent, literal, GOING HARD ALL THE WAY — treats it as a specification. The fatal flaw is diligence. The cure
is communication. [Viktor's chain](./drunken-smith.md) IS the cure — see THE RACE in his doc. Say it how it is,
or bury what you loved.

**Reward family: her gear line** — and, at the end, either the strongest named blade in the game or the first
masterwork of the new way. Depends entirely on whether the old man learned to talk in time.

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `side-001` + early Viktor engagement

### 1) Her Own Mark: `???`
Her first original, branded piece — forged WITH the duo. They source her materials (fetch list TBD — modest,
mid-tier) and work the forge alongside her. Her maker's mark goes on steel for the first time. She will not
stop grinning about it for two quests.

#### After effects of "Her Own Mark"
- Metal Petal stocks her first piece.

### 2) Validation: `???`
She presents the piece to Viktor. He commends it — and critiques it: "obviously good work, but look at this
handle? The weight is all wrong. 8/10." (If Viktor's "Say It Plain" is done, the critique arrives WITH an
actual explanation — and the difference matters more than any player will realize at the time.) Stock upgrades
regardless: 8/10 from the master is 8/10.

#### After effects of "Validation"
- Metal Petal stock tier up (her line, revision 2).

### 3) Something Missing: `???`
She takes the feedback — overhearing, cross-referencing, grinding. New materials run (fetch list TBD — better,
farther), new forging session, new piece, new review. Viktor, weighing it: "oh, this feels much lighter in
hand... but something is missing from the blade. The soul."

He means: *put your heart into it.* She hears: **soul. Component. Missing.**

And she does what she does with every problem the shop has ever had: she researches it.

#### After effects of "Something Missing"
- Stock tier up again (her line, revision 3 — genuinely excellent now).
- Her idle dialogue gains one line about "old smithing texts." Nobody thinks anything of it.

### 4) The Soul of the Craft: `???`

> ch4 zones). Available in the same window as Viktor's remaining quests. Order decides which scene plays.

She asks the duo for one more materials run — the strangest list yet, and a stone tablet from [TBD location].
The player reads it as quest N+1 of a beloved chain. It is not.

**IF VIKTOR'S CHAIN IS INCOMPLETE — the tragedy:**
Viktor wanders in mid-forge: "whatcha making? I didn't know you've been at it."
Viskra: "I will prove myself to you! This is it — you CAN'T DENY MY CRAFT anymore!"
She holds up the sword and chants the words only Viktor recognizes — the words from the tablet the player
fetched — and her body collapses to the floor, soulless, the blade radiating with power unknown.
Viktor is flabbergasted. A tear trickles down his cheek as he watches his brightest pupil unknowingly
sacrifice herself to forge the strongest named sword in the game: **the "Claidheamh Soluis"**
(`Weapons.json [8]` — ⭐⭐⭐, a blade of pure energy; the Sword of Light, containing the sunniest soul in
Raevula, which is exactly why it's the strongest — she went hard, all the way).

Aftermath:
- Viktor cannot bear to keep it: **"Just fucking take it — I can't."** The player receives the Claidheamh
  Soluis.
- **The Metal Petal closes forever.** Viktor exits the game — not at the pub, not anywhere. The forge is
  locked. All his unfinished quests are marked MISSED.
- The duo does not joke. There is nothing to say. (The comedy engine meets the one thing it can't process —
  and the player did the fetch quests.)

**IF VIKTOR'S CHAIN IS COMPLETE — averted:**
Viktor, entering: **"Oh, at it again?"** — because she isn't doing it in secret. She's working openly, for the
shop, and she KNOWS the legend now — the true price, taught to her plainly by a man who finally learned to say
what he means. The tablet is research, not a recipe she intends to follow. She forges her masterpiece the
mortal way — and with the new way Viktor worked out, it reaches heights the old way claimed only death could buy.

Aftermath:
- Her masterwork joins the shop (her line, final revision — name TBD, HER name on it, soul intact).
- The Metal Petal thrives: two smiths, one forge, actual communication.
- Nobody in the game ever knows what almost happened. Except Viktor. And the player who did it in the other
  order, on another save file, and has to live with both.
