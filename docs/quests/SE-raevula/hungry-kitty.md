# The Hungry Kitty

## Location
Raevula, wandering the southeast section, judging everyone.

## About
The cat's name is Mittens. Outer presentation: "Meow?" Inner monologue (canon): "What the fuck are you looking
at, shitbag?" The most honest resident of Raevula. Tier: FLAVOR — the thesis ("everyone is the protagonist of
their own story") extended to its logical extreme: even the cat. Especially the cat.

Interlock: [Nyancy](../NW-raevula/cat-woman.md) translates, if asked. Asking is a mistake the player should be
allowed to make repeatedly.

**Reward family: cat tribute** — small luck-flavored rewards and the grudging tolerance of a superior being.

## Quests

### 1) Tribute (I): `???`
Mittens is hungry. Mittens is always hungry. Feed the cat — but Mittens has STANDARDS, and standard kibble gets
the inner-monologue review it deserves. Requires an actual cooked dish (Yelena/cooking interlock — the cooking
system's most demanding critic weighs in).

### 2) Tribute (II): `???`
A better dish. The review is harsher, because now Mittens knows the player can do better. (The food chain system
sees its highest-stakes deployment: a cat's opinion.)

### 3) Tribute (III): `???`
The finest dish the player can currently craft. Mittens eats the entire thing, sits, stares, and delivers the
highest praise available to catkind: "You may continue to exist, shitbag." Reward: **Feline Favor** — a small
LUK passive, and Mittens occasionally appears in Raevula sitting next to things worth noticing.

#### After effects of "Tribute (III)"
- Mittens follows the duo around the SE quadrant at a dignified distance, pretending not to.
- Nyancy claims full credit for the friendship. Mittens' inner monologue disputes this at length.
