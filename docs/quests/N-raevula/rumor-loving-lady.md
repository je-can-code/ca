# The Rumor-loving Lady

## Location
Raevula, the middle-north section (Map335), standing outside the vacant plot where the nature-lover used to
live — which is, notably, the exact plot Treis's lab gets built on (see
[the scientist](../NE-raevula/scientist.md)). She has front-row seats to the biggest construction gossip of the
decade and she knows it.

## About
The rumor-loving lady's name is Amy. Her own words: "I only just arrived" — and yet she somehow knows everything
about everyone. She is the town's switchboard: who's feuding, who's drinking, whose kid won't let anyone in the
house. **Buried truth:** the rumors are how a newcomer buys belonging. She doesn't collect stories because she's
nosy; she collects them because knowing things is the only currency she arrived with. Nobody has ever asked her a
single question about herself.

**Reward family: information** — map secrets, points of interest, whisper-network breadcrumbs. Amy is the GOSSIP
channel of the Subterranean discovery doctrine (Silia is the personal channel).

## Quests

### 0) Unlock Quest: `main-003`
Rumors need material. Once the mayor is dead and the town is buzzing, Amy is in her element and will flag down
the two strange adventurers everyone keeps talking about.

### 1) Tell Me Everything: `???`
Amy trades in rumors, and the duo are walking rumor-generators. She wants an exclusive: something she doesn't
already know, verified firsthand. Bring her proof of something from beyond the walls (e.g., a Stonefur hide —
Yelena interlock — or a sketch of the basin depths). In exchange, she marks a couple of points of interest on the
player's map (a Suspicious Crack, a Durable Post spot).

#### After effects of "Tell Me Everything"
- Amy's idle dialogue begins tracking story progress with alarming accuracy.
- One or two optional map secrets get marked.

### 2) The Vacant Lot: `???`
The plot she stands beside is her white whale: WHY did the nature-lover abandon a perfectly good house? She wants
the story from the source. Go ask the hippie in the Forest of Dreams and bring back the truth (the mayor's "evil
vibes" — which, post-Wyatt, is retroactively excellent judgment).

#### After effects of "The Vacant Lot"
- When Treis's lab construction begins on the lot, Amy has EXTENSIVE feelings, live, per construction stage.
- She starts treating the player as a peer, not a source.

### 3) Whispers From Below: `???`
> The Sloth vector — gossip channel. Gate: post-snakerope.

Amy has been collecting a category of story she doesn't retell: miner stories. The deep shaft. The ones who went
too far down and didn't come back. She doesn't gossip about these — that's the tell that they're true. She'll
share what she's gathered with the only two people in town strong enough to matter: where the old-timers say the
deep entrance is, and why nobody strings lights down there anymore.

#### After effects of "Whispers From Below"
- The Subterranean's whisper-network breadcrumb is planted (door, never contents).
- Whisper density in town rises after Nimbus is cleared (escalation doctrine).
